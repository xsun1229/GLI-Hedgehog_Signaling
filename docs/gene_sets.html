TADA (autism): Fu et al. 2022

Fu et al. 2022, Nat Genet, Supplementary Table 11. It is the largest and most recent autism TADA analysis: 63,237 individuals, with de novo, inherited and case-control variants and CNVs. TADA gives every gene a false discovery rate; we split them into tiers using the paper’s own thresholds.

Why not the advisor’s High list? The advisor’s file has a 120-gene TADA “High” list but no matching “Med” list, and the source of High is unknown. It does not match any published TADA list we checked. Fu 2022 is the closest:

NDD genes: Wang et al. 2022

Wang et al. 2022, PNAS, Dataset S6. This updates Coe et al. 2019 (Eichler lab) with 46,612 parent–child trios: 15,560 autism and 31,052 developmental delay.

How the genes were found. Only de novo mutations are used: mutations present in the child but in neither parent. For each gene, the observed count of damaging de novo mutations is compared with the number expected from the gene’s mutation rate. Three statistical models are used (chimpanzee–human divergence, denovolyzeR, DeNovoWEST).
